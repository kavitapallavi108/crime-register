The lockdown wave

Each line is one state's cases from 2020 to 2022. Lines that slide down lost cases, lines that climb gained them. Hover or tap a line to read it.

Read before use

Before you share a number from this page

  1. Don't start a trend in 2020. That year is swollen by lakhs of cases filed for breaking COVID lockdown rules.
  2. A high rate can be good news. It may mean people can register an FIR easily, not only that there is more crime.
  3. Arrests are not cases. States count cases registered. Cities count people arrested. Never compare the two.

Sources

National Crime Records Bureau, Crime in India 2020 (Table 19B.2, persons arrested in metropolitan cities) and Crime in India 2022 (Table 1A.1, IPC cognizable crimes by state and UT). ncrb.gov.in

"Cases" are IPC cognizable cases registered. "Chargesheeted" means police finished investigating and sent the case to court. Crime and chargesheeting rates are published for 2022 only. City arrest figures for 2021–22 are estimated from each state's trend.
